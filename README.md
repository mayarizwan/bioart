# Reassortment Cube

An interactive 3D model of my BioArt sculpture for BIOL105L about influenza A and antigenic shift.

**View it here:** https://mayarizwan.github.io/bioart/

A Rubik's cube stands for the flu virus. Its 8 corners are the virus's 8 RNA segments, and twisting the cube mixes them the way two flu strains swap genes when they infect the same cell. Crayons on magnets are the spike proteins: warm colors for hemagglutinin (HA) and cool colors for neuraminidase (NA). Swapping in a new spike shows antigenic shift, which is how flu pandemics start.

Made by Maya Rizwan.
